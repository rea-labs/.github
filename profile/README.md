# REA.lab

**Real-life audio and auditory research**

We study how the brain and mind construct auditory experiences, combining psychoacoustics, neuroimaging, spatial audio, and real-time signal processing. Our research spans hearing loss, tinnitus, hyperacusis, and neurodiverse auditory perception.

Based at the University of Zurich, Department of Otorhinolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery.

### Research software

- [Audio Test Battery](https://github.com/rea-labs/audio_test_battery) - Python-based audiometry and tinnitometry.
- [MNE-RT](https://github.com/rea-labs/mne-rt) - Real-time M/EEG processing, neurofeedback, and BCI. Automatically synchronized fork of [mne-rt-org/mne-rt](https://github.com/mne-rt-org/mne-rt).

Open science, reproducible methods, and hearing research beyond the laboratory.
